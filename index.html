FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which channels can I connect?
Telegram, Instagram, Facebook Messenger and WhatsApp. All conversations from connected channels appear in a single WQ-Sender inbox.
Do I need programming skills?
No. Connecting channels and the flow builder are completely no-code — everything is set up in a visual dashboard.
How do automated flows work?
You build a funnel from messages, buttons and conditions. When a customer writes to any channel, the flow runs automatically — from a simple reply to a full conversation without an operator.
What about broadcasts and the customer database?
The service automatically collects contacts from all channels into a single database. You can segment your audience by tags or channel and launch broadcasts.
When will the AI agent be available?
The AI agent is in development. It will learn from your business knowledge base and conversations and handle chats 24/7. Message us to be the first to know about the launch.
How much does it cost?
Pricing is tailored to the needs of your business. To find out the cost, get in touch — we'll advise you and find the best solution.
How do I get started?
Message us on Telegram or at info@wq-sender.com — we'll help you connect and set up your first flow.
