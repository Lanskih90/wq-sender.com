Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the WQ-Sender service (the “Service”, “we”) collects, uses and protects information about users and the data processed while operating the Service. By using the Service, you agree to the terms of this Policy.

1. What data we collect

Depending on how you use the Service, we may process the following categories of data:

  • Account data — email address and contact details provided during registration or when contacting us.
  • Connected channel data — tokens and identifiers of bots and pages (Telegram, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp) that you connect yourself.
  • End-customer data — messages, names, usernames, identifiers and language of users who write to your connected channels.
  • Technical data — information about the use of the Service required for its proper operation and security.

2. How we use data

  • to provide and support the functionality of the Service (unified inbox, flows, customer database, broadcasts);
  • to process and route messages between connected channels;
  • to improve the quality, stability and security of the Service;
  • to contact you regarding the operation of the Service and support.

3. Roles of the parties

With respect to end-customer data, the account owner (the business) acts as the data controller, and WQ-Sender acts as the processor, processing the data on behalf of the business and solely for the purpose of providing the Service.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell personal data. Data may be shared only with: infrastructure providers necessary for operating the Service; messaging platforms within the scope of message delivery; and in cases required by law.

5. Storage and protection

We take technical and organizational measures to protect data from unauthorized access, loss or disclosure. Data is stored for as long as necessary to provide the Service or as required by law.

6. Your rights

You have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of your data, as well as to restrict its processing. To exercise these rights, use the contacts below. Step-by-step instructions for deleting data are available on the User Data Deletion page.

7. Cookies

The website may use strictly necessary cookies and local storage for the interface to work properly. The wq-sender.com landing page does not use advertising trackers.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. The current version is always available on this page with the date of the last update.
