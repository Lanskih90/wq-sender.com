User Data Deletion

Last updated: August 11, 2026

WQ-Sender is an omnichannel platform for handling requests from Telegram, Instagram, Facebook Messenger and WhatsApp. This page explains how to request deletion of data that WQ-Sender processes in connection with the use of the app, including data received via Facebook and Instagram. General processing rules are described in our Privacy Policy.

1. How to submit a deletion request

Choose any convenient channel — both are equally valid:

  • Email: info@wq-sender.com — with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  • Telegram: @WQ_SENDER_bot — send a message with the text “Data deletion request”.

So that we can find and delete exactly your data, please include in your request:

  • the name or ID of the connected Facebook Page or Instagram account;
  • the email address your WQ-Sender account is registered to (if any);
  • what exactly should be deleted: all account data or the data of a specific connected channel.

We send a confirmation of receipt within 2 business days and a separate confirmation once the data has actually been deleted. Before processing the request, we may ask you to confirm that you are authorized to manage the respective Page or account — this protects against deletion of data by third parties.

2. What data is deleted

  • access tokens and identifiers of connected Pages, bots and accounts;
  • message history and attachments received via connected channels;
  • end-customer profiles: names, usernames, identifiers, language, tags and notes;
  • flow, automation and broadcast list settings associated with this account;
  • contact details of the account owner.

3. Timelines

Data is deleted within 30 days of the request being confirmed. Backups in which data may technically remain are overwritten according to the regular rotation cycle and are not used to provide the Service.

4. What is retained after deletion

We are required or compelled to retain a certain minimum of information:

  • payment and accounting records — for the period established by the tax legislation of Ukraine;
  • anonymized technical logs and statistics — without the ability to identify a specific person;
  • the deletion request itself — as proof that it was fulfilled.

5. Removing the app yourself

You can revoke WQ-Sender’s access to your Facebook data at any time:

  • open Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites;
  • find WQ-Sender in the list and click “Remove”.

This stops the app from accessing your data going forward but does not delete what has already been stored in WQ-Sender. To delete stored data, submit a request as described in section 1.

6. If you are a customer of a business, not an account owner

With respect to end-customer data, the account owner (the business) acts as the data controller, and WQ-Sender acts as the processor acting on its behalf. If you wrote to the messenger of a company that uses WQ-Sender, please contact that company directly first. You can also write to us using the contacts below — we will forward the request to the relevant business and help get it fulfilled.
